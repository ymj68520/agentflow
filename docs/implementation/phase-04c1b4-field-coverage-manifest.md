## Diagnostic-only exclusions

No required proof field is excluded in this interim inventory. Human-readable `DimensionProof.reason` and `ActionGuardCompatibility.reason` are presentation text, not verdict identity; they may be omitted from byte-for-byte comparison only after their normative role is checked and the remaining proof fields are compared. No exclusion is used to hide a missing Oracle. Source line numbers, Debug text and console order are not present as formal report fields and are outside the semantic projection.

## Counts and closure

The complete recursive field total has **not been mechanically closed** because the type graph contains recursive control trees, variants, optional witnesses and context-dependent collections. Required semantic field count, independently derived count, full-field compared count and uncovered count are therefore **not certified**. The known uncovered families above are sufficient to keep the phase at Outcome D. The 68 comparator mutations validate mismatch detection only; they do not increase Oracle-derived or conformance-compared field counts.
