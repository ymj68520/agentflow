# Phase 4B.2C — Thirteen-opcode audit matrix

Formal schemas are in `spec/semantic/semantic-ir.md` §§10–13; the closed
opcode/ownership table is authoritative. “Per child” means provenance is
retained, not flattened into one cross-component coordinate. All summaries
remain derived-only and inherit non-Proven child obligations.

| Opcode | Formal schema, owner and static inputs | I/O and Effect (`epsilon`) | Failure (`Phi`) and Usage (`U`) | Flow, Recovery/K, Resource and X; outcomes/identity | Production path, Conformance, remaining gap |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| INVOKE | `InvokeBody(contract, inputs, resources)`, zero children; exact component contract pin and proof context. | Exact input/output port and type bindings; closed/open/unknown `E` and scoped occurrences copied; ResourceRequirement ports map to caller ResourceSlotIds. | Checked typed Failure row and meter/phase Usage envelope; no absence→empty/zero. | F and effect-flow partitions, exact K/action+usage bindings, R requirements, X profile and proofs are source-scoped. Outcome is component success or declared typed Failure, not dispatch. | AWAIT | `AwaitBody(signal_type, correlation)`, zero children; exact TypeSchemaRef and Phase 3C correlation expression. | No ComponentContract interaction adapter; no business Effect is invented. | SEQUENCE | `children[N>=1]`, ordered owner edges. | Existing checked accumulation; later positive lower bounds become zero when continuation is not proven; upper and source occurrences retained. | RACE | At least two first-terminal branches, common interface. | Accumulates every contestant's E, including losers; only unguaranteed lowers weaken. | Failure union; U sums every possible contestant. | Flow winner is unknown; all contestant K and resource/X facts retained, including loser recovery. | `computation-control/race/*`. | ITERATE | Closed `foreach`/`while` body plus carry. | RETRY | Ordered finite delay list; child-computation retries, max attempts `1+len(delays)`. | E envelope is checked-scaled by max attempts; occurrence family records logical and physical attempt bounds. | U scales by attempts, including `U.recovery`; Failure is not removed. | K is checked for retry safety; forbidden replay violates, other evidence can be RequiresEvidence; sources remain per child. | `computation-advanced-control/retry/*`; `retry-forbidden-replay`. | TIMEOUT | Child plus duration expression; no timer state. | Child E remains unchanged; no timeout discount. | Child Failure/U preserved; TimedOut is a distinct outcome, not Phi. | Timeout-vs-child decision is unknown; K/R/X remain per child. | HANDLE | Protected child plus exact typed Failure/TimedOut clauses and handlers. | Protected E plus mutually exclusive handler branch join and no-handler path; handler E remains conditional. | Exact Failure residual; unmatched failures remain. | SCOPE | `ScopeBody(child, resources)`, exactly one owned subtree; resource set exists, unique and sorted. | Child E/guards/bounds/occurrences are copied; cancellation does not reduce upper. | Child Phi and U copied; Forward/Recovery phases stay separate. | Phase 3C parent/declaration/child ResourceSlot comparison; Flow/K/X preserved. | CALL_SUBFLOW | `CallSubflowBody(contract, inputs, resources)`, zero local children; exact Subflow SemanticIrDigest target. | Exported interface/Effect envelope is resolved from explicit snapshot; no body is inferred from name. | Exported Phi/U/K remain from exact checked export. | Exported Flow/R/X and source mapping stay exact where available; body conformance remains RequiresEvidence absent trusted evidence. | `resolve_computation_leaf`; `computation-leaf/subflow/*`. Coverage runners: 58 structured-control cases, 72 advanced-control cases, and
55 Scope/Compensation/root cases (the latter covers the full opcode set across
its trees). See the cross-dimension and counterexample matrices for uncovered
cross-products; a passed opcode row alone is not cross-dimension closure.
