capture, full Scope integration and activation are separate acceptance milestones.

be a future registered stage-ownership change; no assumption that a planning T3

root order and owner tuple. A verified old complete carrier may be compared as
old data; importing it as new requires complete real source values and explicitly
authorized fresh successor revalidation/binding. New carrier ranks must be
derived under new registered content, not mapped by old numbers/shape/hash.

history or absent association/rank binding stay nonmigratable. A fresh declared
source is fresh input, not restoration of an old invocation. C1/C2/R3-CTX-REL
bindings remain registered; no Native snapshot storage/equality or port verdict
change is proposed. F03 attribution/dependency closure is not closed by a full
manifest field projection. Native Full Seed, Standalone and other producers do
not gain automatic access to the new stage or a new profile root roster.
